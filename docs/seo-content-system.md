# Public SEO and Content System

Status: PLANNED.

## Intended audiences

Indonesian affiliate creators, niche publishers, small affiliate teams, and shoppers comparing products before purchase. English content can be enabled for selected categories when search intent and editorial capacity justify it.

## Public site information architecture

- Home: explain the value proposition, show curated categories, editorial guides, and a clear creator sign-up CTA.
- Categories: useful, differentiated category landing pages with curated products and buying criteria.
- Product pages: verified specifications, pros and cons, price/availability timestamp where sourced, disclosure, comparison links, and editorial review date.
- Comparisons: explicit methodology, criteria, alternatives, and a clear update history.
- Guides: educational tutorials, buyer guides, use cases, troubleshooting, and product-selection advice.
- Editorial hub: authors, categories, tags, publish/update dates, related content, and editorial policy.
- Trust pages: about, contact, privacy, terms, affiliate disclosure, corrections policy, and editorial methodology.

## Keyword planning schema

Each keyword record should include primary phrase, normalized phrase, language, country/locale, topic cluster, parent topic, related terms, long-tail query, search intent (informational/commercial/transactional/navigational), target page type, audience, funnel stage, proposed title, meta description, slug, content brief, SERP notes, source of volume/difficulty data, last checked date, and status.

Search volume and keyword difficulty must be nullable unless an actual data source is connected and measured. Do not fabricate precise figures. Where there is no data source, prioritize using qualitative opportunity labels based on specificity, intent fit, business relevance, and observed search results.

## Ten starting content clusters

1. Affiliate marketing strategy and business models.
2. Affiliate platform and network comparisons.
3. Product reviews and buyer guides.
4. Product-versus-product comparisons.
5. SEO keyword research and content planning.
6. Affiliate link management and campaign tracking.
7. Social media promotion and short-form content.
8. AI-assisted image, video, and copy workflows.
9. Conversion optimization, analytics, and reporting.
10. Compliance, disclosures, privacy, and operational finance.

Each cluster should be expanded into specific subtopics only after validating relevance, search results, audience questions, and available first-party or user-provided data. Avoid generic filler pages and doorway pages.

## Editorial lifecycle

Idea -> intent classification -> keyword clustering -> source collection -> brief -> draft -> factual/SEO review -> affiliate disclosure and compliance check -> human approval -> publish -> internal-link pass -> measure -> refresh or retire.

## SEO acceptance criteria

- Content answers a defined user question and matches its intent.
- Product claims, specifications, prices, and promotions have a source and checked date.
- Affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly near relevant recommendations and according to applicable rules.
- Page has a unique title, useful meta description, descriptive headings, accessible media alt text, canonical URL, and relevant internal links.
- Images and videos have usage rights and do not misrepresent products.
- Human editorial approval is required before public publishing.
