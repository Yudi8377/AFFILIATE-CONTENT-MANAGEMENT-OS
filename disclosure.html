TRANSPARENCY & TRUST

Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Some public product pages may include affiliate links. If a visitor purchases through such a link, the publisher may receive a commission without changing the price paid by the visitor. The presence of a commission does not guarantee that a product is suitable for every visitor.

Product claims and pricing

Product descriptions, availability, and prices can change. Check current terms directly with the merchant before making a purchase. The publisher should only make claims supported by reliable evidence and should clearly distinguish verified facts, opinions, estimates, and unverified claims.

Reviews and recommendations

Recommendations should disclose material relationships, avoid fabricated testimonials, and explain relevant limitations. A paid relationship or affiliate commission must not be presented as independent evidence of product quality.

Use of generated content

Template-based or AI-assisted drafts require human review. Do not publish unsupported performance claims, fake search-volume metrics, synthetic endorsements, or imagery that misrepresents a real product. Verify commercial usage rights for any uploaded or generated media.

Questions and corrections

For corrections or concerns, use the repository issue tracker. Do not include private customer information in public issues.
